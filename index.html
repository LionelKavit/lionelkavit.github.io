My story

The analyst clients counted on,
now building AI you can count on.

01 · The analyst — 2021–2024

At Enfusion (now Clearwater Analytics) I managed buy-side client accounts for three years. When a P&L looked off, a valuation needed defending, or a trade broke, clients escalated to me — fund accounting, performance, valuation, and risk across multi-asset portfolios. Beyond answering the questions, I automated OMS and PMS workflows, managed market-data vendors, and trained junior analysts.

02 · Graduate school — 2024–2026

Then I went to build the other side. At UW–Madison I learned to engineer agentic AI and shipped it outside finance to prove the skill travels. For MSA Professional Services, I built agent-run ETL pipelines in Microsoft Fabric that documented a large enterprise dataset, with copilots on top so users could query the data in plain English. For a manufacturing capstone, I built an Azure AI decision-support tool that automated the statistical analysis step in a steel-parts process.

03 · Now

Same instinct, new tooling: be the one people can rely on. I build AI agents with the guardrails and evaluation to earn that reliance — data pipelines and cloud deployment included. Spring 2026 graduate, open to relocation.
